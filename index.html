02 SPACE X CAPSTONE PROJECT

SPACEX • FALCON 9

Falcon 9 First-Stage Landing Prediction

A complete data science project focused on analyzing SpaceX Falcon 9 launch data and predicting whether the first stage of a Falcon 9 rocket would successfully land. The project follows a practical data science workflow, beginning with data collection and web scraping and continuing through data wrangling, analysis, feature preparation and machine-learning modeling.
